# Logo Usage

## Official mark
Use the Controlled Passage with breaks mark only.

## Correct usage
- Keep original proportions.
- Use official blue `#2563EB`.
- Use white or dark bracket strokes depending on background.
- Keep clear space around the mark.

## Wrong usage
- Do not use the continuous-line logo.
- Do not remove the middle breaks.
- Do not stretch, rotate or recolor.
- Do not add shadows, glow, gradients or 3D effects.
