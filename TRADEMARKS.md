# RouteGate Trademarks and Brand Assets

AGPL-3.0-or-later covers the repository software and project documentation
unless a file or directory states otherwise. It does not grant permission to
use RouteGate trademarks.

## Protected Materials

Protected RouteGate identity materials include:

- The RouteGate name.
- RouteGate logos, icons, marks, and visual identity.
- Product, service, and project names that identify RouteGate.
- Brand artwork and other materials in brand asset directories.

## Permitted Uses

You may use the RouteGate name in a truthful, non-confusing way to:

- Refer to the RouteGate project.
- Describe compatibility with RouteGate.
- Identify that your work is based on or derived from RouteGate.
- Preserve required notices and attribution.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, official status, or control
by the RouteGate project unless you have written permission.

## Official and Unofficial Builds

Unofficial builds, forks, packages, appliances, hosted services, integrations,
and managed services must not imply that they are official RouteGate releases
or endorsed by RouteGate.

Modified builds should clearly identify themselves as modified and unofficial.
They should use a distinct name and visual identity so users can tell who built
or operates them.

Do not use names such as "RouteGate Enterprise", "RouteGate Pro", "Official
RouteGate", "Certified RouteGate", "RouteGate Appliance", or similar confusing
names without written permission.

Official builds, signed releases, support offerings, appliance packaging, and
managed RouteGate may be provided separately by RouteGate maintainers or
authorized parties.

## Restricted Uses

Do not use RouteGate trademarks or brand assets to:

- Name a fork, distribution, product, service, company, or domain in a way that
  could confuse users about source or affiliation.
- Suggest that an unofficial build, package, service, or integration is official.
- Modify official logos or brand assets except as allowed by an applicable
  brand asset license or written permission.
- Remove or alter trademark, copyright, or attribution notices.

## Forks and Modified Versions

Forks and modified versions should use a distinct name and visual identity.
They may state that they are based on RouteGate when that statement is accurate
and not misleading.

This trademark boundary does not restrict your AGPL-3.0-or-later rights to
inspect, modify, fork, or redistribute the code. The goal is to protect users
from confusing or malicious builds while keeping the software open.

## Brand Asset License

Some brand asset directories may include their own license or usage notes. Those
terms apply to the assets in that directory and are separate from the software
license.
